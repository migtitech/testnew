Our services

Collection and recovery for retail lending portfolios

Calling, field and pickup services for card, loan and vehicle portfolios across Madhya Pradesh. Every mandate below is one we have handled for a bank or NBFC.

01

Credit card collections

We handle card portfolios from the first missed payment to written-off balances. The calling team works on early buckets, and field staff take over harder accounts in the branch towns.

Coverage

  • X to 30 bucket
  • X to write-off
  • Settlement follow-up
  • Skip tracing

Lenders

  • SBI Card
  • ICICI Bank
  • Axis Bank
  • OneCard

02

Personal & business loan collections

Collections and recovery on unsecured personal loans, cross-sell loans and small business loans. Accounts are followed up by phone, then by a field visit where needed.

Coverage

  • Personal loans
  • Business loans
  • Cross-sell loans
  • Recovery

Lenders

  • IDFC FIRST Bank
  • Aditya Birla Finance
  • Hero FinCorp
  • Fullerton India
  • DMI Finance
  • Lending Card

03

Two-wheeler, car & used-car loans

EMI collection and recovery on vehicle loans. Branch teams know the dealers, the towns and the customers, which helps accounts get resolved faster.

Coverage

  • Two-wheeler loans
  • Car loans
  • Used-car loans
  • FCPU & DR

Lenders

  • Bajaj Auto Finance
  • Hero FinCorp
  • Tata Capital
  • Wheels EMI
  • IDFC FIRST Bank
  • ICICI Bank

04

Consumer finance & pay-later

Small-ticket consumer loans need quick, polite contact. We run these portfolios mainly from the calling floor, with field support where the lender allows it.

Coverage

  • Consumer durable loans
  • EMI debit card dues
  • Pay-later recovery
  • Postpaid collection

Lenders

  • ICICI Bank
  • Kotak Mahindra Bank
  • Paytm

05

Field visits & pickup collection

Doorstep pickups for home, personal, two-wheeler and auto loans. Staff collect under the lender's receipt process, and every pickup is deposited and reported.

Coverage

  • Doorstep pickup
  • Receipting
  • Deposit & reconciliation
  • FCPU & DR

Lenders

  • ICICI Bank
  • Hero FinCorp

06

Tele-calling setup

The Indore office runs 35 calling seats under two team leaders. Calls cover reminders, promise-to-pay tracking and contact updates, with every conversation logged for the lender.

Coverage

  • 35 calling seats
  • 2 team leaders
  • Call logging
  • Daily MIS

Lenders

  • Available for all portfolios
