About us

A collection agency run the way a lender would run it

Hardiya Associates is a proprietorship based at Silver Mall, Indore, providing collection, recovery and customer contact services to banks and NBFCs in Madhya Pradesh.

Our objective

To earn a lasting place in the service industry by providing effective, efficient and quality work in customer relations, collection, recovery and data management.

Our early experience was in card collections for SBI Card and GE Countrywide cash cards. Today we handle credit cards, personal and business loans, two-wheeler and auto loans, consumer durables and pay-later products for fifteen lenders.

The work has changed over the years, but the approach has not: clear allocation, trained people, documentation for every account and reporting that a lender's collections manager can act on straight away.
