How we work

From allocation to reconciliation

A simple, accountable process that lenders can audit at every step.

  1. Allocation & planning

    Accounts are sorted by bucket and location, then given to the team for that portfolio.

  2. Calling & reminders

    The tele-calling floor contacts customers, logs every conversation and records payment commitments.

  3. Field visits & pickups

    Branch staff follow up in person, collect payments and issue receipts under the lender's process.

  4. Reporting & deposit

    Collections are deposited and reconciled, and MIS is shared with the lender's team.

Frequently asked questions

Does Hardiya Associates give loans?

No. We are a collection and recovery agency. We work on behalf of banks and NBFCs to follow up on existing credit card and loan accounts. For a new loan or card, please contact the lender directly.

Which lenders do you work with?

Our mandates have covered SBI Card, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, IDFC FIRST Bank, Bajaj Auto Finance, Hero FinCorp, Aditya Birla Finance, Tata Capital, Fullerton India, DMI Finance, Paytm, OneCard, Lending Card and Wheels EMI.

Which areas of Madhya Pradesh do you cover?

We run our corporate office in Indore and have 21 branch locations, including Ujjain, Dewas, Ratlam, Mandsaur, Neemuch, Khargone, Khandwa, Gwalior, Jabalpur and Chhindwara. The full list is on our Branches page.

I received a call about my dues. How do I check it is genuine?

Ask the caller for their name, the lender and your account details, then confirm directly with your bank or NBFC using the number printed on your statement or card. Always make payments through the lender's official channels or against an official receipt.

Can you take on a new portfolio at short notice?

Yes. We keep separate teams for separate portfolios and can rearrange staff when a new allocation arrives. Share the product, bucket, volume and locations through the contact form and we will get back to you.

Looking for a collection partner in Madhya Pradesh?

Tell us about the portfolio, bucket and locations. We will come back with a plan for coverage, staffing and reporting.
