## 3. Review strategy (this is what actually moves local ranking)

Reviews from lender-side contacts and staff of partner branches are legitimate; fake reviews
are not, and Google filters them. Aim for a steady trickle — five reviews in one afternoon
looks manufactured.

Ask each reviewer to mention the city and the service naturally, for example:
"Worked with their team on two-wheeler loan collections in Ujjain."

Reply to every review, positive or negative, within a few days. Never mention a customer's
account number, dues or personal details in a public reply — that is a privacy breach.
For an angry borrower, reply with something like:

```
Thank you for writing to us. We work strictly within the code of conduct set by the lender
we represent. Please call our office at 0731-4979841 and we will look into your specific
concern.
```
